Made by hand

Every stitch by hand, one at a time.

Worked with a hook, never a machine. The texture is the record of the making.

01 The Yarn

Cotton ribbon, soft in the hand and heavy enough to hold a shape without lining or stiffener. Chosen for how it behaves after a year of use, not how it looks on day one.

02 The Stitch

Worked entirely by hand with a hook. The bobble stitch eats yarn, which is why the surface has depth that machine work cannot reach.

03 The Finish

The top edge is braided closed, the ends are worked back into the body, and the brass plaque is set last. Nothing is glued.
