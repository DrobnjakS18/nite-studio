# Surface: index.html (whole site)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: Belgrade Instagram follower on a phone choosing one of five one-of-one crochet bags, then ordering by Instagram DM.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, BAGS array drives all products, single page (no product pages), current photos kept as-is (no cut-outs yet), Serbian copy, no cart. Keep NITÉ wordmark, manifesto and "Ručna izrada". Drop per-bag colour bands and brass. User pinned references: Polène handbags listing, Lulu Guinness collection page, Fendi women's bags.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A maison catalogue, not a storytelling scroll: the page opens directly on the five pieces as a quiet product grid, the way Polène and Fendi list bags. Refuses the logo hero and the five identical colour bands.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, #f4f3f1 product tiles, near-black ink, one grey for meta, 1px #e6e4e0 hairlines. Bodoni NITÉ wordmark and a light Bodoni page title; everything else tiny uppercase Inter with wide tracking. Square tiles, no radii, no shadows, black full-width order bar.
STORY: The visitor sees all five bags at once, opens one, reads material, size and price, and taps one black bar that opens an Instagram DM with the bag name to mention. Manifesto and "Ručna izrada" follow as quiet editorial blocks; the page closes on a giant NITÉ wordmark.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin header (links left, centred NITÉ, Instagram right) over a left-aligned Bodoni "Torbe" title with one grey line, a rail of five model thumbnails with names, a hairline toolbar reading "5 komada", and the top of the grid: one 2×2 feature tile beside four singles on desktop, a full-width lead tile on mobile.
FORM: user-pinned catalogue grid (Polène/Lulu Guinness/Fendi), overriding the roll; seed key d2183c0e. Signature interaction: tile hover crossfades to the second view; the tile opens a full-screen product panel (gallery left, sticky info right) deep-linked by hash.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
